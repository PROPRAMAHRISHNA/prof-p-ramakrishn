Industrial R&D

Selected product and process innovations.

The record documents approximately 25 new products/processes developed at CFTRI and Tata Tea, with more than 10 commercialized.

Red chilli fractionation

Process developed for beneficiation of the pungent portion.

Black pepper cleaning, washing & drying

Continuous washing and drying system developed and demonstrated.

Garlic powder manufacture

Continuous dehusking and husk separation method developed; released to seven parties.

Instant coffee

Fixed counter-current battery and pilot high-pressure process conditions standardized for 46% yield on R/G coffee.

Instant tea extraction

Preferential leaching process developed to obtain good flavour in the end product.

Pectin from apple pomace

Concentration and precipitation process developed, including spray-drying conditions.

Seed dehulling

Mechanical dehulling system developed for muskmelon, watermelon and allied fruit seeds.

Tea innovation portfolio

Flavoured tea, decaffeinated tea, cold brew tea, green tea, RTD tea, vitamin tea, tea cubes, fast brew tea and flavoured green tea.

Energy management in tea

Waste-heat recovery improved thermal efficiency by 25%.

Tea process controls

Microprocessor-based controls were tried for tea manufacture.
